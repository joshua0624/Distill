# What I want to watch

This feed is for entertainment as much as learning. Don't treat "fun" as low substance:
a good game recap or gaming video is exactly what I'm here for.

## Strong interests
- Tech and AI: podcasts, interviews and talks with builders and researchers; AI coding tools
  and workflows (Claude Code and similar); hands-on walkthroughs of new models and dev tools.
- Basketball: the Houston Rockets above all, then the NBA in general: full games, highlights,
  film breakdowns, trade and draft analysis, player interviews.
- Football: the Miami Dolphins above all, then the NFL in general, same formats.
- Gaming: VALORANT and Rocket League: pro matches, VOD reviews, high-level gameplay,
  patch and meta breakdowns, entertaining gaming creators.
- Learning: well-made explainers and courses on science, engineering, math and how things work.

## Some interest
- Other sports and other games when the video is well made or a big event.
- Longer conversations and documentaries on topics adjacent to the above.

## Not for me
- Reaction videos, compilations, and "top 10" listicles padded to length.
- AI voiceover channels, reuploads, and text-to-speech slideshows.
- Drama, gossip and rage bait, including sports "hot take" shouting shows.
- Clickbait whose title or thumbnail promises more than the video delivers.
- Generic news recaps with nothing new, and filler made to hit a length.
